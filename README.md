Leadership Portfolio Redesign:
  
-> Overview

This project was completed as part of the DeepThought Product Management Internship Assignment.

The objective of this redesign is to transform the existing Leadership Portfolio from a data-heavy monitoring dashboard into a narrative-driven career artifact that employees can proudly share with mentors, managers, and their future selves.
Instead of presenting isolated metrics, the redesigned experience focuses on storytelling, growth, contribution, impact, and future trajectory while preserving data fidelity and organizational context.

 -> Problem Statement

The current Leadership Portfolio presents performance data in a dashboard-like format. While it provides metrics, it lacks:

* Clear information hierarchy
* Personal identity and ownership
* Narrative flow
* Meaningful interpretation of data
* Shareability outside the organization
As a result, employees see numbers but not their story.

-> Proposed Solution

The redesigned Leadership Portfolio follows a **Career Pitch Deck** approach.

Key Principles

* Story First, Data Second
* Strong Top-Level Signal
* Growth-Oriented Presentation
* Clear Information Hierarchy
* Portable & Shareable Design
* Learnings Over Limitations

-> Portfolio Structure

The redesigned experience is organized into six core sections:

1. Executive Summary
2. Contribution Highlights
3. Capability Growth
4. KPI Impact
5. Challenges & Learnings
6. Career Trajectory

This structure helps users understand:

- What they achieved
- How they grew
- What challenges they faced
- Where they are heading next

-> Design Decisions

 1. Lead With a Strong Signal

The first screen answers:

> "How did I perform this month?"

This creates immediate clarity and confidence.

 2. Narrative Before Metrics

Users first understand the story of their month before diving into detailed numbers.

 3. Growth-Focused Experience

The design highlights progress, learning, and career trajectory rather than only monthly outputs.

 4. Positive Constraint Framing

Challenges are presented as learning opportunities rather than complaints.

 5. Portable Career Artifact

The portfolio is designed to be understandable even outside the organization.

-> Challenges Addressed

* Data Overload
* Metrics Without Meaning
* Lack of Career Narrative
* Difficulty Sharing Outside the Organization
* High Cognitive Load
